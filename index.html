The problem we want to address

Food waste shouldn't be the end of the story.

Food waste decomposing in landfills without proper management can release methane into the atmosphere. Methane Alleviator explores another approach: managing domestic food waste and making use of the products of decomposition.

Our mission is to help alleviate the environmental effects of methane released from landfills, produce biogas that could serve as an alternative cooking fuel, and create organic fertilizer that could substitute conventional chemical fertilizer.

Designed with purpose

Meet the key features.

Each feature has a specific role in food-waste processing, monitoring, resource recovery or garden care.

⚙️

Food Waste Shredder

Cuts and grinds domestic food waste into smaller particles, increasing the surface area for decomposition by aerobic bacteria.

🪴

Two-Layer Fertilizer Collection

Waste decomposes in the top container for two weeks, then moves to the bottom container for further decomposition. The process takes up to one month.

☀️

Solar Panel

Designed to convert solar energy into electricity. The report specifies an installation angle of approximately 18°–36° to absorb solar radiation.

🌡️

Smart Monitoring Sensors

Temperature, moisture and pH sensors monitor conditions in the fertilizer compartment and provide alerts when readings are outside the set range.

💧

Rainwater Irrigation

An ultrasonic sensor monitors the rainwater tank level. The system is designed to use stored rainwater when available and switch to household water when it is low.

🌬️

Odour Control

A detachable, replaceable mesh bag containing sodium bicarbonate granules is installed under the cover to help absorb unpleasant odours.

The process

How Methane Alleviator works.

A staged process designed to manage food scraps, collect fertilizer and route biogas through a filtration and storage system.

STEP 01
🥕

Shred the Waste

Domestic food waste enters through the funnel and is cut into smaller particles.

STEP 02
🧫

Decompose in Stages

Waste stays in the top container for two weeks before moving to the bottom layer for further decomposition.

STEP 03
🧪

Filter the Biogas

The designed filter uses steel wool, activated carbon and water to remove selected unwanted components from the gas.

STEP 04
🌱

Collect Useful Outputs

After up to one month, fertilizer can be collected. The gas-storage design uses an inner tank floating inside an outer water-filled tank.

This describes the intended design in the report. Actual compost quality, gas purity, gas safety and performance require appropriate testing and validation.

More than a composter

Thoughtful details for a better system.

❄️

Liquid Cooling Mechanism

The cooling system is designed to help control overheating and direct methane-containing gas toward the filter and storage tank.

🧹

Excess Water Removal

A water-removal hole helps drain excess liquid during shredding to prevent excessive moisture in the fertilizer collection compartment.

🧲

Biogas Filtration System

The report describes steel wool for hydrogen sulphide removal, activated carbon for water droplets, and water to dissolve some carbon dioxide.

🛢️

Floating Gas Storage Tank

The inner tank rises as gas enters and lowers as gas is used, with the design intended to compensate for changes in stored gas volume.

🚨

Alerts and Monitoring

The control centre is designed to alert users when the last fertilizer container is ejected and when monitored conditions need attention.

🚿

Water Circulation

The irrigation tank circulates water to help prevent stagnation and reduce the risk of mosquito breeding.
